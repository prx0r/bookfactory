# Foldout reference — learn to prefer the script by using it

## Vowels
| IAST | Independent | After क |
|---|---|---|
| a | अ | क (*ka*, inherent a) |
| ā | आ | का |
| i | इ | कि |
| ī | ई | की |
| u | उ | कु |
| ū | ऊ | कू |
| ṛ | ऋ | कृ |
| ṝ | ॠ | कॄ (rare) |
| ḷ | ऌ | कॢ (rare) |
| e | ए | के |
| ai | ऐ | कै |
| o | ओ | को |
| au | औ | कौ |

An explicit virāma suppresses the inherent vowel: क् = *k*. Combined clusters use half forms or conjunct ligatures: त् + म = त्म (*tma*), स् + त = स्त (*sta*). A phoneme and its handwritten glyph are distinct from a philosophical deity-correspondence.

## Consonants organised by articulation
| Place | Unaspirated voiceless | Aspirated voiceless | Unaspirated voiced | Aspirated voiced | Nasal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Velar | क ka | ख kha | ग ga | घ gha | ङ ṅa |
| Palatal | च ca | छ cha | ज ja | झ jha | ञ ña |
| Retroflex | ट ṭa | ठ ṭha | ड ḍa | ढ ḍha | ण ṇa |
| Dental | त ta | थ tha | द da | ध dha | न na |
| Labial | प pa | फ pha | ब ba | भ bha | म ma |

Approximants: य ya, र ra, ल la, व va. Sibilants: श śa (palatal), ष ṣa (retroflex), स sa (dental). Aspirate: ह ha. Anusvāra ं (ṃ) and visarga ः (ḥ) are signs requiring contextual phonological explanation; they are not interchangeable with every nasal or with an English h.

## Script progression
- Lessons 1–2: see IAST, trace Devanāgarī, verify by reference.
- Lessons 3–4: write before looking, no time pressure.
- Lessons 5–7: print Devanāgarī first and reveal IAST after attempt.
- Lessons 8–10: read directly from the Sanskrit line, then use the reverse derivation as answer key.

## The difference that matters in Trika
अनु *anu* (after/following) is not अणु *aṇu* (minute, in some Trika usages the limited individual). Dental न and retroflex ण are distinct sounds and letters. The theological meaning of *aṇu* is learned from Trika sources, not derived from the look of the glyph.

## Mnemonic nyāsa option
Traditional Mātṛkā body placement is offered as an **optional linked companion** only after the specific recension, phoneme order, bodily positions and textual source have been checked.
