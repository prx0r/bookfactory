# Why the redesign is better

1. **Rasa is an editorial organising analogy, not a sourced nine-state healing technology.** Abhinavagupta's aesthetics concerns aesthetic relish and has a complicated predecessor history. This restores precision.
2. **Each verse remains complete.** Utpaladeva's long syntactic prayers are read whole; a cut is only a labelled memory handle after collation. No half-line has been presented as a new mantra.
3. **One act, one turn, one return.** The reader has something simple to do without needing bodily signs or contrived ritual performance.
4. **Textual authorship is explicit.** Ghaṭakarpara is not automatically credited to Kṣemarāja. Sanskrit from machine-proposed L4 remains HELD.
5. **Each emotion includes moral reality.** Fear may call for safety, anger may justify boundaries, and grief may need company. Nonduality must not become an excuse to bypass action.
6. **Reader-facing copy contains no invented Sanskrit.** The JSON overlays existing entry IDs and refuses to replace canonical witness text.
