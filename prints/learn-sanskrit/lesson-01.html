mamātmā — My Self

Abhinavagupta, Bhairavastava v.2 (extract); translation-work bhairavastava · ref Bhairavastava bh-p02 · id l01-mamatma · section stage-1-sounds

Witness: Abhinavagupta, Bhairavastava 2 — translation-work/bhairavastava (gold; GRETIL/Muktabodha M00505)

Variants: Focus extract: tvaṃ ca maheśa sadaiva mamātmā. Full verse continues svātmamayaṃ mama tena samastam.

1 · Original witness

tvaṃ ca maheśa sadaiva mamātmā
IAST (Deva transliterated at build)

Translation: You, Maheśa, are ever my own self.

2 · Recitation

tvaṃ ca | ma-he-śa | sa-da-i-va | ma-mā-tmā

Vowels: ā in mamātmā from a+a→ā; long e in maheśa; ai in sadaiva from a+e→ai
Articulation: retroflex ṣ in maheśa; ṁ anusvāra in tvaṃ
Metre: Bhairavastava verse; free philosophical cadence

Rule box 1 — a + a / a + ā → ā
Adjacent short a (or a + ā) contract to one long ā. Pāṇini 6.1.101 akaḥ savarṇe dīrghaḥ. Reverse: split ā into a + a when the words demand it.
Rule box 2 — a + e → ai
yaṇ sandhi: sadā + eva → sadaiva; brahma + eva → brahmaiva. Reverse: ai may hide a + e.

7 · Syntax

  • Case roles: tvaṃ (nom); maheśa (voc); mamātmā (nom predicate); tvadanugrahaśaktyā (instr means)
  • Agreement: mamātmā nom.sg.m agrees with tvaṃ
  • Finite verbs: bhāti (3sg) in full verse; extract is predicate clause
  • Implied: copula implied in “You … are … my Self”

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: You · and · Maheśa(O) · always-alone · my-Self

Literal: You, Maheśa, are always my own Self.

Readable: You, Maheśa, are ever my own self — made of You, all is mine through You. (full L2 continues)

Alternatives: No competing parse of mamātmā; other sites stay open when they arise.

9 · Trika exegesis

Abhinavagupta / text: Bhairavastava is a hymn of grace; the extract isolates Maheśa as one’s own Self.

Bridge: Nominal predication mirrors identity claim — identity is hymn theology, not dictionary ātman.

  • Maheśa (voc) — address to Śiva as great lord
  • mamātmā (nom) — Self as “mine” — possession into identity
  • anugraha-śakti (instr) — grace as means of the seeing

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary for this line; house mealtime lore is Gītā 4.24.

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing only; L2/L3 from translation-work.

10 · Practice and mastery

  • Recite: Recite the extract three times; feel a+ā fuse.
  • Write: Write mama and ātmā separately, then fused with the rule.
  • Memorise: Hold sadā+eva→sadaiva as rule box 2.
  • When: Opening prayer of the volume.

Transfer exercises (unseen / labelled):

  • Reverse mamātmā → two words + rule.
  • Why does ātmā keep long ā?
  • Find another a+ā fusion later.

How: Extract for a+ā; full verse for grace as instrumental.
