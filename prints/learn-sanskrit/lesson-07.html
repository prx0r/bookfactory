tādātmyam anayor … vahnidāhikayor iva — the dual

Abhinavagupta, Bodhapañcadaśikā v.3; translation-work bodhapancadasika · ref Bodhapañcadaśikā bod-p03-v03 · id l07-dual-fire · section stage-4-trika-grammar

Witness: Abhinavagupta, Bodhapañcadaśikā 3 — translation-work/bodhapancadasika (gold)

Variants: Duals: anayor, vahnidāhikayor (gen/loc dual).

1 · Original witness

śaktiś ca śaktimadrūpād vyatirekaṃ na vāñchati | tādātmyam anayor nityaṃ vahnidāhikayor iva ||
IAST (Deva transliterated at build)

Translation: Power wants no parting from her bearer — never, ever; the two are eternally one — as fire is one with burning.

2 · Recitation

śak-tiś ca | śak-ti-mad-rū-pāt | vy-a-ti-re-kaṃ | na vāñ-cha-ti | tā-dāt-myam | a-na-yor | ni-tyaṃ | i-va | va-hni-dā-hi-ka-yor

Vowels: ā in tādātmyam, dāhi
Articulation: ñ in vāñchati; ṣ in śakti; ḍ in tādātmyam
Metre: aphoristic pair

Rule box 6 — dual gen/loc = -yor
Exactly two: anayor, vahnidāhikayor. Not plural -ānām / -eṣu.

7 · Syntax

  • Case roles: śaktiḥ nom; vyatirekam acc; śaktimadrūpāt abl; tādātmyam nom pred; dual gen/loc -yor
  • Agreement: tādātmyam n.nom.sg; duals limit the pair
  • Finite verbs: vāñchati 3sg negated
  • Implied: iva pulls fire–burning standard

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: Power-and-ca · from-power-possessor-form(ABL) · difference(ACC) · not-desires · identity · of-these-two · eternal · as · of-fire-burning-two

Literal: Power also does not desire separation from the form of the power-holder. Identity of the two is eternal, as of fire and burning.

Readable: Power wants no parting from her bearer — never, ever; the two are eternally one — as fire is one with burning.

Alternatives: anayor dual; referents from context (śakti & bearer) in syntax.

9 · Trika exegesis

Abhinavagupta / text: Śiva–Śakti identity through fire–burning — dual does the work of “exactly two that are not two.”

Bridge: Dual introduced on Trika’s central analogy; -yor makes metaphysics more exact.

  • śakti / śaktimat — power and bearer of power
  • anayor (dual) — the pair that is identity
  • vahnidāhikayor (dual) — fire and burning as irreparable pair

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary here.

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing; L3 from translation-work.
