1 · Original witness

nṛtyati gāyati hṛṣyati gāḍham saṃvit iyam mama …
IAST (Deva transliterated at build)

Translation: This consciousness of mine dances, sings, rejoices deeply — O Bhairava, Lord.

7 · Syntax

  • Case roles: three verbs same person/number; saṃvit nom subject; iyam nom f; mama gen
  • Agreement: saṃvit f.nom.sg with iyam
  • Finite verbs: nṛtyati, gāyati, hṛṣyati — 3sg present active
  • Implied: asyndeton between verbs

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: dances · sings · rejoices · deeply · consciousness · this · mine

Literal: Dances, sings, rejoices deeply — this consciousness of mine.

Readable: This consciousness of mine dances, sings, rejoices deeply — O Bhairava, Lord: having won You, my dear…

Alternatives: iyam as f.nom with saṃvit; layer “indecl.” flag is artifact.

9 · Trika exegesis

Abhinavagupta / text: saṃvit is the grammatical subject of dance/song/joy — not the ego alone.

Bridge: Three -ti endings first; subject consciousness second; philosophy follows grammar.

  • saṃvit (nom) — consciousness as the one who dances
  • nṛtyati/gāyati/hṛṣyati — manifestation as play
  • gāḍham — depth of relish (rasa-adjacent)

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary here.

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing; L3 from translation-work.
