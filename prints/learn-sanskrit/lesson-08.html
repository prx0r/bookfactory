1 · Original witness

alaṃ bhedānukathayā tvadbhaktirasacarvaṇāt
IAST (Deva transliterated at build)

Translation: Enough of difference-talk — from chewing the sap of devotion to you.

2 · Recitation

a-laṃ | bhe-dā-nu-ka-tha-yā | tvad-bhak-ti-ra-sa-car-va-ṇāt

Vowels: ā in bhedānukathayā (a+a→ā)
Articulation: ṃ in alaṃ; -kth- in anukathā; ṇ in carvaṇāt
Metre: philosophical line

Rule box 7 — alaṃ + instrumental = enough of
alaṃ governs the instrumental: alaṃ bhedānukathayā. Substitutes are labelled exercises.

7 · Syntax

  • Case roles: alaṃ+instr frame; bhedānukathayā instr; tvadbhaktirasacarvaṇāt abl cause
  • Agreement: instr.f -ayā; abl -āt
  • Finite verbs: none in extract
  • Implied: “Enough of X” — no finite verb

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: enough · difference-talk(INSTR) · your-devotion-sap-chewing(ABL)

Literal: Enough with difference-talk; from chewing the sap of devotion to you.

Readable: Enough of difference-talk. From chewing devotion's sap for you —

Alternatives: bheda+anukathā standard in L0 citation.

9 · Trika exegesis

Abhinavagupta / text: Names bheda as discourse to stop; devotion-rasa is the cause clause.

Bridge: Instr and abl do the work; metaphysics waits for Lesson 9.

  • bheda — habit of keeping subject/object apart
  • bhakti-rasa (abl) — affect as means of recognition

Lakshmanjoo: Not primary here.

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing; L3 from translation-work.
