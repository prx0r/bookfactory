1 · Original witness

paraṃ parasthaṃ gahanād anādim | sarvālayaṃ sarvacarācarasthaṃ tvām eva śambhuṃ śaraṇaṃ prapadye ||
IAST (Deva transliterated at build)

Translation: Supreme, abiding beyond, depthless, beginningless, one — lodged in many caves, home of all: Śambhu, to You I come for refuge.

7 · Syntax

  • Case roles: epithet chain facing tvām; śaraṇam acc; prapadye 1sg mid; gahanād abl
  • Agreement: acc masc/n; śambhum masc acc
  • Finite verbs: prapadye only
  • Implied: refuge frame “I take refuge in You alone”

8 · Translation from the syntax

Interlinear: supreme · beyond-abiding · from-depth · beginningless · one · all-abode · all-moving-unmoving-abiding · you-acc · indeed · Śambhu-acc · refuge-acc · I-resort

Literal: Supreme, abiding beyond, from the depth, beginningless, one, entered manifoldly in caves, abode of all, abiding in all moving and unmoving: You alone, Śambhu, refuge — I resort.

Readable: Supreme, abiding beyond, depthless, beginningless, one, lodged in many caves, home of all: Śambhu, to You I come for refuge.

Alternatives: passages header vs v01 locator noted — no silent renumbering.

9 · Trika exegesis

Abhinavagupta / text: First act is refuge in Śambhu after absolute epithets; v.3 names author-view.

Bridge: Acc marks what is taken; non-dual claim comes later in the work, not into the case table.

  • śaraṇam (acc) — refuge as what is entered
  • prapadye (1sg mid) — self-involved resorting
  • epithet chain — qualities of the one in whom refuge is taken

Lakshmanjoo: Lakshmanjoo mealtime is a different text (Gītā 4.24).

Our synthesis: Pedagogical framing; L3 from translation-work.
