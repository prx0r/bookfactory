Metrics from live `prints/practical-prayers/entries.json` vs
`editorial/` candidate (same 36 ids; Sanskrit/source fields byte-identical).

## Why that is better (editorial contract)

1. **No promised sensations.** The old copy told readers what their body must
   do (“gratitude with heat”, “small heat in the face”). The new standard
   forbids scheduled bliss and failure states — the practice is the honest
   act, not a feeling check. Prevents shame when nothing “arrives.”
2. **Every page answers When → Act → Return.** Readers can use a prayer in
   10 seconds without decoding mysticism. Live copy often buried the moment
   of use inside prose.
3. **Shorter, scannable facing pages.** −38% guide text leaves room for
   Sanskrit and fits the 108pp budget without shrinking type.
4. **Speech-act honesty.** Pocket lines are labelled EXTRACT; modern
   “use this in an argument” is labelled adaptation, not Āgama. Stops the
   book inventing ritual.
5. **Attribution stays split.** Gītā ≠ Abhinavagupta’s commentary ≠
   Lakshmanjoo’s meal custom — candidate `howto` names the teacher’s
   practice instead of implying the verse commands water-in-palms.
6. **Still warm.** Tone goal from `00_explainer_standard.md`: concrete and
   reverent, not clinical. The gold pages (Gītā 4.24, alaṃ bhedānukathayā)
   show the model.

## What is *not* solved by the English overlay

Source QA (`05_source_qa.csv`) still has **12 BLOCK / 4 CHECK / 20 REVIEW**
(e.g. Somānanda ref, Dehastha renumber, truncated Śivastotrāvalī 16.1,
Bahurūpa recension). **Do not treat prettier English as a witnessed text.**
