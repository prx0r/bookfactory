Śivasūtra 1.1

Consciousness is the Self

caitanyam ātmā

Consciousness is the self.

Words

surfacelemmaanalysis
caitanyamcaitanyaneuter nominative singular abstract noun, -ya derivative of cetana
ātmāātmanmasculine nominative singular; stem ātman → nominative ātmā

Proof steps

  • caitanya + -am → caitanyam (nominative sg)
  • ātman → ātmā (masculine nominative sg)
  • no overt verb: Sanskrit permits a nominal equation with understood asti

The turn

The Sanskrit establishes an identification, not a Cartesian proof that any individual stream of experience owns the universe. Distinguish caitanya (consciousness) from mere citta (mental contents).

When: with this lesson. Practice: recite, then reverse one derivation. Return: Which word is masculine, and which neuter? Does the equation require both to have the same gender?
