The Un-Drunk One's Thirst

भवद्भक्त्यमृतास्वादाद् बोधस्य स्यात् परापि या / दशा सा मां प्रति स्वामिन् नासवस्येव शुक्तता
bhavadbhaktyamṛtāsvādād bodhasya syāt parāpi yā / daśā sā māṃ prati svāmin nāsavasyeva śuktatā
may the higher knowledge come from tasting the nectar of your devotion —that state, lord, turn toward me,keen as the not-yet-drunk one's thirst for the first drink.
1
The Guide

The Un-Drunk One's Thirst

Utpaladeva, Śivastotrāvalī 1.11 · 1.11
The poem does not shame longing.
What is it

The poem does not shame longing. It asks that devotion, rather than possession, become the taste of longing

How to perform it

When longing is loud: say the verse and let the object of the longing become the taste of devotion rather than the thing you'd grab.

How to know it's working

Return: Do the next honest thing about the desire; pursue or decline it without making the outcome a spiritual verdict.

What to feel / visualise

keep the ache — it is the rasa before it arrives; the thirst IS the practice.

The Wine of Non-Difference

kṣaṇam apīha na tāvakadāsatāṃ prati bhaveyam ahaṃ kila bhājanam / bhavadabhedarasāsavam ādarād avirataṃ rasayeyam ahaṃ na cet
let me not spend one moment here as anythingother than your servant's vessel — one who reverently,unceasingly drinks the rasa-wine of your non-difference.

What Delight, Beloved

अम्बुदैः शिखिगणो विनाद्यते का रतिः प्रिय मया विनाद्य ते
ambudaiḥ śikhigaṇo vinādyate kā ratiḥ priya mayā vinādya te
by clouds the peacock-flock is sounded —what delight, beloved, without me today?

The Guide

What Delight, Beloved

When longing for someone or something: say it as the peacock line — the cry is appropriate, beautiful, and will end with the rain. Feel the ache as weather: you are the bird, not the drowning.

What to feel / visualise

The Moon That Carries the Nectar

प्रकाशां शीतलाम् एकां शुद्धां शशिकलाम् इव / दृशं वितर मे नाथ काम् अप्य् अमृतवाहिनीम्
prakāśāṃ śītalām ekāṃ śuddhāṃ śaśikalām iva / dṛśaṃ vitara me nātha kām apy amṛtavāhinīm

the heat stays but stops being an emergency.
